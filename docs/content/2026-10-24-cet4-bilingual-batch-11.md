### 第1题

【答案】C · His son had ordered food from several restaurants.
【定位】短篇新闻1第1、2段：孩子用父亲账户下单。
【关键英文（节选）】“used his father's online food ordering account to order food from one restaurant after another”
【中文释义】男孩使用父亲的网上点餐账户，接连从不同餐馆订食物。
【解析】账单高达1000美元，是因为六岁儿子借用手机后没有玩游戏，而是用父亲账户向多家餐馆下单。
英文对应：“His son had ordered food from several restaurants.” is supported by “used his father's online food ordering account to order food from one restaurant after another”.
【辨析】A He had bought meals with his online food ordering account.：父亲并非自己购买餐食；报道说是儿子使用其账户。
B His son had used his cell phone to pay for playing a game.：手机原本是给孩子玩游戏的，但没有说他用手机付款购买游戏。
D He had dined out at a famous five-star restaurant.：没有说父亲去五星餐馆用餐；食物是外送到家。
【说明】本项目编写的学习解析，非官方。

### 第3题

【答案】A · They examined how visitors affected animal behaviour in zoos.
【定位】短篇新闻2第1段：研究调查的范围。
【关键英文（节选）】“They examined how visitors affected the behaviour of more than 250 species in zoos.”

D They examined what behaviours indicated animal boredom.：重复行为可能是无聊信号，但研究范围远不止识别这类行为。
