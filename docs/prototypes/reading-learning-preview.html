A Meeting with Time to Think

留出思考时间的会议

英文内容与配套素材仅用于此次原型预览

Before a student organization planned its annual event, its committee met in a crowded room. The same two people usually spoke first, and their ideas often shaped the whole discussion. Quieter members sometimes sent useful suggestions afterward, when the main decision had already been made.

一个学生组织为年度活动开会时，总是同样的两个人率先发言，他们的想法常常影响整个讨论。比较安静的成员有时会在主要决定作出之后，才提出有用的建议。

For the next meeting, the chairperson shared a short agenda in advance. Each topic included a question and the decision the group hoped to make. Members could read the material beforehand and send notes if they wished.

下次会议前，主席提前分享了一份简短议程。每个议题都列出一个问题和小组希望作出的决定，成员可以提前阅读材料，并自愿提交笔记。

At the beginning of the meeting, the group spent a few quiet minutes writing ideas. Members then compared notes in pairs before the wider discussion. This arrangement did not make every contribution equally useful, but it gave more people a clear point at which to begin.

会议开始时，大家先安静地写了几分钟想法，然后两人一组交流，再进行全体讨论。这并没有让每个意见都同样有用，但给了更多人明确的参与起点。

The chairperson also changed the questions used during discussion. Instead of asking whether an idea was “good,” she asked what problem it solved, what resources it required, and what information was still missing. A short summary at the end made the next steps clear.

主席还改变了讨论问题的方式。她不再只问想法是否“好”，而是询问它解决什么问题、需要什么资源，以及还缺哪些信息。最后的简短总结明确了接下来的行动。
